The chain

Three screens, always in this order

1Peer · 30 minA teammate audits your work. Everyone starts here — team leads included.
2Team Lead · 20 minAn assigned lead re-audits the same day, the same accounts.
3Manager approvalOnly after approval does the score reach your scorecard.

The system enforces the order. A lead cannot score before the peer has, and nothing counts until it's approved.

Say: Three screens. Nobody skips a stage, and no score touches your scorecard until it clears all three.

The accuracy rule

A careless review costs both of you

The team lead audits the exact same day and accounts. If they find problems you passed, the gap is charged to the reviewer and the person reviewed.

✓Within 3 pointsForgiven. Honest judgement differs — that's expected.
!Beyond thatDemerit points on both people, scaled to the gap, up to 2.0 each.
→Both are toldA notification names the gap and the reason. Nothing happens quietly.

Example: peer scores 96, lead scores 84 → gap 12 → 1.0 demerit point each.

Say: Passing everything to be kind is not kind. If the lead finds what you missed, you both take the hit.
