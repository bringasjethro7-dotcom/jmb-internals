Delivery — your posting board

Check your phones first, then post

1Phones by computerYou only see your own posting phones. Tap a phone to cycle Connected → Disconnected → Has an issue. Out-of-play phones are hidden automatically.
2My posting todayYour % and totals, plus Next up: @handle with Jump — that is your resume point.
3Posting boardChips All · Not yet · Posted · On hold. Per account: ♪ audio, fill Post 1–4, then one tick.

"N not active → Fleet" means logged-out or suspended accounts left your board. You can't edit Fleet — tell a lead. Only leads can put a phone On hold.

Narration: Delivery is your posting board. First the banner: check your phones. They are grouped by computer and you only see your own. Tap a phone to cycle Connected, Disconnected, Has an issue. My posting today shows your progress and Next up with a Jump button — that is where you resume. On the board, fill Post one to four for an account, then tick it. If you see "not active, Fleet", those accounts are logged out or suspended — tell a lead, you can't edit Fleet yourself.
